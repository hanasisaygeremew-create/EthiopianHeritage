WHO WE ARE

Connecting People With Ethiopia's Past

Ethiopian Heritage is an educational platform created to help people discover and learn about Ethiopia's remarkable historical sites.

From the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela to the ancient obelisks of Aksum and the royal castles of Gondar, Ethiopia preserves a rich history that deserves to be explored and protected.

🏛️

Our Mission

To make information about Ethiopia's historical places accessible and encourage people to appreciate and preserve our shared heritage.
